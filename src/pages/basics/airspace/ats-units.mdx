## ATS Unit Staffing Policy

Latvia vACC enforces staffing of ATS units in line with the [VATSIM Code of Conduct Section C5](https://vatsim.net/docs/policy/code-of-conduct#c-controller-conduct), [VATSIM Europe Division Policy](https://core.vateud.net), and [VATSIM Europe Division Training Policy](https://core.vateud.net). The following provisions apply to all ATCOs wishing to staff an ATS unit in EVRR FIR as well as to the ATS units themselves.

- ATS units are staffed on a "first come" basis in line with the aforementioned policies.
- Should a position booking be made through official vACC mediums or a roster be published by the vACC Event Department for an event, they shall take priority over any and all ATCOs not listed in said booking/roster. Rosters shall always maintain the highest priority in determining staffing.
- ATC training sessions shall have priority over regular controlling sessions if required by a member of the vACC ATC Training Department.
- ATS units bearing multiple frequencies or sectors shall be staffed beginning with the designated primary sector/frequency. Subsectors (non-primary sectors) and non-primary frequencies may not be staffed unless the primary sector/frequency is staffed or a written exception was issued by either the vACC Administrative Staff or the vACC Event Coordinator.
- All disputes and conflicts regarding staffing of ATS units are resolved by the vACC ATC Training Director or a vACC member to whom such duties and responsibilities have been delegated in writing.
