# TWR SOP – Phraseology

For the purposes of this and all other phraseological examples in this Knowledgebase, IFR traffic will always be denoted with common airline callsigns, such as `BTI123` (Air Baltic 123) and VFR traffic will be denoted with registration-based callsigns, such as `YLKGB` (`YL-KGB`: Yankee, Lima, Kilo, Golf, Bravo).

- `()` parentheses shall be used for optional parts of instructions that are to be used on a case by case basis.
- `[]` square brackets shall be used for instances where a choice must be made by the station speaking on frequency. Different options will be separated with `/` forward slashes.
- `{}` curly braces shall be used for arbitrary values that will change on a case-by-case basis.

## Basic Phraseology

### Takeoff Clearances

  <span>

  </span>
  <span>

    runway 18, cleared for takeoff, bye-bye!`
  </span>
  <span>BTI123: `Runway 18, cleared for takeoff, see you, BTI123.`</span>
</PhraseologyDisplay>

### Missed Approach Instructions

<PhraseologyDisplay>
  <span>
    TWR: `BTI123, GO AROUND, GO AROUND, {REASON}, climb out runway track to
    altitude 2500FT, contact Riga Approach on 129.925.`
  </span>
  <span>
    BTI123: `Going around, climb out runway track to altitude 2500FT, contact
    Riga Approach on 129.925, BTI123.`
  </span>
</PhraseologyDisplay>

<PhraseologyDisplay>
  <span>BTI123: `BTI123, going around, {REASON}.`</span>
  <span>
    TWR: `BTI123, roger, climb out runway track to altitude 2500FT, contact Riga
    Approach on 129.925.`
  </span>
  <span>
    BTI123: `Climb out runway track to altitude 2500FT, contact Riga Approach on
    129.925, BTI123.`
  </span>
</PhraseologyDisplay>

Notes:

- Both Rīga Tower and the aircraft's crew can invoke the missed approach procedure.
- During periods of High Intensity Runway Operations (HIRO), the reason for invocation of the missed approach procedure by the ATS unit may be omitted from the instruction.
- The reason for invocation of the missed approach procedure must be clearly communicated to APS.
- Rīga Tower reserves the right to alter the standard missed approach procedure by issuing a single vector to the aircraft in question in order to provide timely separation or otherwise ensure safe and efficient operations. An example is shown below.

<PhraseologyDisplay>
  <span>
    TWR: `BTI123, GO AROUND, GO AROUND, {REASON}, turn [right/left] heading
    {HEADING}, climb to altitude 2500FT, contact Riga Approach on 129.925.`
  </span>
  <span>
    BTI123: `Going around, turn [right/left] heading {HEADING}, climb to
    altitude 2500FT, contact Riga Approach on 129.925, BTI123.`
  </span>
</PhraseologyDisplay>

<PhraseologyDisplay>
  <span>BTI123: `BTI123, going around, {REASON}.`</span>
  <span>
    TWR: `BTI123, roger, turn [right/left] heading {HEADING}, climb to altitude
    2500FT, contact Riga Approach on 129.925.`
  </span>
  <span>
    BTI123: `Turn [right/left] heading {HEADING}, climb to altitude 2500FT,
    contact Riga Approach on 129.925, BTI123.`
  </span>
</PhraseologyDisplay>

#### Instructions For Airborne Traffic

##### Aerodrome Visual Circuit

<PhraseologyDisplay>
  <span>YLKGB: `YLKGB, airborne, passing 200FT.`</span>
  <span>TWR: `YLKGB, roger, report downwind (with intentions).`</span>
  <span>YLKGB: `Report downwind (with intentions), YLKGB.`</span>
</PhraseologyDisplay>

<PhraseologyDisplay>
  <span>YLKGB: `YLKGB, on downwind.`</span>
  <span>TWR: `YLKGB, roger, report your intentions.`</span>
  <span>YLKGB: `Request touch-and-go, YLKGB.`</span>
  <span>TWR: `YLKGB, roger, report final.`</span>
</PhraseologyDisplay>

<PhraseologyDisplay>
  <span>YLKGB: `YLKGB, on downwind.`</span>
  <span>TWR: `YLKGB, roger, report your intentions.`</span>
  <span>YLKGB: `Request touch-and-go, YLKGB.`</span>
  <span>TWR: `YLKGB, roger, extend downwind till avised.`</span>
  <span>YLKGB: `Extend downwind till advised, YLKGB.`</span>
</PhraseologyDisplay>

<PhraseologyDisplay>
  <span>TWR: `YLKGB, cleared base turn.`</span>
  <span>YLKGB: `Cleared base turn, YLKGB.`</span>
</PhraseologyDisplay>

Notes:

- In low traffic settings, VFR aircraft may be cleared to land, for touch-and-go, or for a low pass from the downwind leg of the visual circuit.

##### Entry to the Controlled Airspace

<PhraseologyDisplay>
  <span>
    YLKGB: `Riga Tower, hello, YLKGB, requesting to enter the controlled
    airspace, entry point ELING.`
  </span>
  <span>
    TWR: `YLKGB, hello, cleared to enter the controlled airspace, entry point
    ELING, remain at or below 1000FT, QNH 1013, join the right hand downwind,
    runway 18, report passing overhead ELING.`
  </span>
  <span>
    YLKGB: `Cleared to enter the controlled airspace, entry point ELING, remain
    at or below 1000FT, QNH 1013, join the right hand downwind, runway 18,
    report passing overhead ELING, YLKGB.`
  </span>
</PhraseologyDisplay>

##### Exit from the Controlled Airspace

The exit clearance is phrased as follows.

<PhraseologyDisplay>
  <span>
    YLKGB: `YLKGB, requesting to exit the controlled airspace, exit point
    KAGAL.`
  </span>
  <span>
    TWR: `YLKGB, cleared to exit the controlled airspace, exit point KAGAL,
    remain at or below 1500FT, QNH 1013, report passing overhead KAGAL.`
  </span>
  <span>
    YLKGB: `Cleared to exit the controlled airspace, exit point KAGAL, remain at
    or below 1500FT, QNH 1013, report passing overhead KAGAL, YLKGB.`
  </span>
</PhraseologyDisplay>

The "frequency change approved" instruction is phrased as follow.

<PhraseologyDisplay>
  <span>YLKGB: `YLKGB, overhead KAGAL.`</span>
  <span>
    TWR: `YLKGB, frequency change approved, flight information service is
    available on frequency 120.225.`
  </span>
  <span>
    YLKGB: `Frequency change approved, flight information service is available
    on frequency 120.225, YLKGB.`
  </span>
</PhraseologyDisplay>

##### Transit of the Controlled Airspace

<PhraseologyDisplay>
  <span>
    YLKGB: `YLKGB, requesting to transit the controlled airspace via the North
    Transit Route.`
  </span>
  <span>
    TWR: `YLKGB, cleared to transit the controlled airspace, entry point MAORI,
    follow the North Transit Route, exit point KAGAL, remain at or below 500FT,
    QNH 1013, report passing overhead MAORI.`
  </span>
  <span>
    YLKGB: `Cleared to transit the controlled airspace, entry point MAORI,
    follow the North Transit Route, exit point KAGAL, remain at or below 500FT,
    QNH 1013, report passing overhead MAORI, YLKGB.`
  </span>
</PhraseologyDisplay>

<PhraseologyDisplay>
  <span>YLKGB: `YLKGB, requesting to transit the controlled airspace.`</span>
  <span>TWR: `YLKGB, report your requested routing.`</span>
  <span>
    YLKGB: `Requesting entry via ARNIS, routing JAMAR, exit via KROGS, YLKGB.`
  </span>
  <span>
    TWR: `YLKGB, cleared to transit the controlled airspace, entry point ARNIS,
    routing JAMAR, exit point KROGS, remain at 1500FT, QNH 1013, report passing
    overhead ARNIS.`
  </span>
  <span>
    YLKGB: `Cleared to transit the controlled airspace, entry point ARNIS,
    routing JAMAR, exit point KROGS, remain at 1500FT, QNH 1013, report passing
    overhead ARNIS., YLKGB.`
  </span>
</PhraseologyDisplay>

Note:

- Runway thresholds may be used as visual references and points in a transit routing.
- Aircraft requesting to overfly the runway must be explicitly cleared to `cross the field`.

##### Provision of Traffic Information

<PhraseologyDisplay>
  <span>

  </span>
  <span>YLKGB: `Traffic in sight, YLKGB.`</span>
  <span>TWR: `YLKGB, roger.`</span>
</PhraseologyDisplay>

### Transfer of Communications

#### Departures

NIL. In case of pilot deviation the following phraseology shall be used.

<PhraseologyDisplay>
  <span>BTI123: `BTI123, airborne.`</span>
  <span>
    TWR: `BTI123, Riga applies the automatic handoff procedure for all
    departures, please check the charts. Contact Riga Approach on 129.925.`
  </span>
  <span>BTI123: `Contact Riga Approach on 129.925, BTI123.`</span>
</PhraseologyDisplay>

#### Arrivals

NIL. In case of pilot deviation the following phraseology shall be used.

<PhraseologyDisplay>
  <span>BTI123: `BTI123, runway vacated.`</span>
  <span>TWR: `BTI123, welcome to Riga, contact Ground (on 118.805).`</span>
  <span>BTI123: `Contact Ground (on 118.805), BTI123.`</span>
</PhraseologyDisplay>

## Advanced Phraseology

### Reduced Runway Separation Minima

#### Takeoff Clearances

<PhraseologyDisplay>
  <span>
    BTI123: `Riga Tower, BTI123 ready for an immediate departure from G.`
  </span>
  <span>
    TWR: `BTI123, Riga Tower, surface wind 120 degrees, 6 knots, runway 18,
    cleared for an immediate takeoff, bye-bye!`
  </span>
  <span>
    BTI123: `Runway 18, cleared for an immediate takeoff, see you, BTI123.`
  </span>
</PhraseologyDisplay>

<PhraseologyDisplay>
  <span>BTI123: `Riga Tower, BTI123 ready for departure from G.`</span>
  <span>
    TWR: `BTI123, Riga Tower, preceding traffic is vacating the runway (via Y),
    surface wind 120 degrees, 6 knots, runway 18, cleared for takeoff, bye-bye!`
  </span>
  <span>BTI123: `Runway 18, cleared for takeoff, see you, BTI123.`</span>
</PhraseologyDisplay>

Notes:

- During periods of High Intensity Runway Operations (HIRO), the pilots are expected to contact Rīga Tower using their callsign only. Rīga Tower may explicitly prompt a given crew for their ability to depart immediately.
